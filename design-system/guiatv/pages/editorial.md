## Editorial home
- Lead story (bigger) → secondary stories → latest → categories. Not identical weight for all cards.
- Category signal via `--accent-editorial` sparingly + typography, not saturation.

- Measure ≤ 70ch, line-height 1.6, clear h2/h3, responsive imagery, breadcrumb, author/date/category, related content, share row.

## Rankings
- Ordinal rank, title, image, metadata, concise description; consistent rating treatment.
- Discoverable from global "Más" sheet AND blog context nav (paths unchanged: `/editorial/rankings`).
